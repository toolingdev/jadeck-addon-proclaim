# Changelog

## 1.1.0

Needs jadeck 0.5.0 or newer for the new widgets (add-on SDK v0.3.0).

- **New widgets:** Live slide and Next (slide pictures; tap Next to take
  it), Stage screen, Service clock, Rundown (the service by section),
  Slides of this item, Scripture on screen, Song parts, Notes, Quick
  screens, Video & audio, Service sections and a Button pad of your own
  Proclaim buttons. The widgets have their own settings.
- **New actions** (also macro steps and agent tools): go to slide, take
  next, start pre-service / warm-up / service / post-service, hide / show
  text, video play / pause / restart / back / ahead, next audio, quick
  screens (built-in and the service's own), song parts, show a Bible
  passage, go on / off air.
- **Setup help:** Proclaim PCs on this computer's networks are listed in
  the address field; Test answers in one line and says what to change in
  Proclaim when something is off.
- Slide pictures are served through the controller (no direct browser
  access to Proclaim needed).
